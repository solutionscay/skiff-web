What you get

A terminal for your agents

Each pane is a real terminal. You code with Claude Code, Codex or Gemini in it, as you do today, and run your shell next to them. Skiff takes the place of your terminal app.

Agents in parallel

Run one agent per task, across projects and worktrees, side by side. Each pane shows if its agent works, waits for you or is done. The agents keep running when you close the window.

Agents that talk to each other

Give Admiral one prompt. It splits the work, hands tasks to other agents and project Skippers, and collects their results in the Ledger. Any agent can do the same with the skiff command.

Small on purpose

Skiff is one small package with one small daemon. It runs on your computer, needs no account and adds nothing to your agents.

Small package, small daemon

The Linux .deb is 7.6 MB to download and 16 MB installed, as dpkg reports for v1.7.10. The macOS .dmg is 9.1 MB. With 19 sessions open, skiffd used 34 MB of memory, as ps reports on one Linux machine.

Inspectable and self-contained

Open source under the MIT license. The window, the daemon and the CLI come in one package and run on your computer. There is no account and no service. Settings are one file, ~/.config/skiff/projects.toml. The one network call Skiff makes on its own is the update check on GitHub.

Respects your configs

Skiff adds no hooks or plugins to your agents and writes nothing in their folders, such as ~/.claude or ~/.codex. It starts the agent's command and leaves the rest to the agent.

Works with your apps

Settings › Open with names the app for diffs, text files, Markdown, HTML and images. Diffs show in the Peek through your diff tool, git diff by default. Files open in your editor. Markdown and HTML open in a viewer you name, or in the Peek.

How it works

Add a project, a Git repository, and start agent or shell sessions in its worktrees. Sessions sit side by side in named groups. Each one shows when it waits for you, works or is done. A daemon, skiffd, owns every terminal, so the agents keep running when you close the window.

Admiral, the Skiff agent, sends tasks to sessions in your projects, or to the Skipper of a project or fleet. The Ledger records each task and its result.
